# Reference App

## Purpose

Demonstrates realistic use of the shared UI/UX platform with fake business scenarios.

## What Belongs Here

- Reference flows for transactions, approvals, lookups, validation, desktop shell, and mobile shell.
- Fake modules used to prove platform behavior.

## What Does Not Belong Here

- Real client business rules.
- Real client integrations.
- Client-specific deployment code.

## Allowed Dependencies

- Shared platform packages.
- App framework dependencies when the reference app is implemented.

## Examples of Future Exports

- Fake sales invoice flow.
- Fake mobile approval flow.
- Fake scanner capture flow.
